Good to know
  • Preorder only. Nothing is available same-day or walk-up.
  • Limited quantities each week. Once the drop sells out, it's closed until the next one.
  • Prepayment required to hold your spot.
  • Delivery is local only. We deliver within 10 miles of Arlington and don't ship. Someone needs to be available to receive the order.
  • The pickup window is firm. Orders not collected within the window are forfeited and can't be refunded or rescheduled.
  • No substitutions or custom flavors during the fall season.
